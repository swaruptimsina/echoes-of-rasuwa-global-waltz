# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in **Echoes of Rasuwa: Global Waltz** a harassment-free, scientifically rigorous, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive scientific and open-source environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect toward disaster-affected communities depicted in case studies (Rasuwa, Pantanal, San Joaquin, Hatay, Alaska–Yukon).
- Upholding strict scientific honesty regarding satellite data provenance, uncertainty bounds, and model limitations.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical and scientific feedback.

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, fabrication of scientific data or granule identifiers, and misrepresentation of educational simulations as operational emergency warning systems.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or scientifically dishonest behavior may be reported by opening a confidential issue or contacting the repository maintainers.
