# Official Challenge Resource Integration Map (`Dancing with the SARs`)

Every official space-agency dataset, portal, guide, and tutorial provided by **NASA**, the **Canadian Space Agency (CSA)**, and **CONAE (Argentina)** for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge *"Dancing with the SARs"* is mapped below to its concrete implementation in this repository.

| # | Official Resource | Agency | Official URL | How & Where Used in Repository |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NASA Earthdata Search** | NASA | `https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/search?q=NISAR` | Dynamically deep-linked per AOI bounding box via `buildEarthdataSearchDeepLink()` in `src/services/nisarCatalogService.ts` and accessible in `src/components/AnalystWorkbench.tsx` and `src/components/ResourcesDrawer.tsx`. |
| 2 | **NASA ASF Data Search Vertex** | NASA | `https://search.asf.alaska.edu/#/?dataset=NISAR` | Queried via the NASA CMR API (`provider=ASF`) for all 6 AOIs; bundled snapshot at `public/nisar-catalog/cmr-snapshot-2026-11-13.json`; WKT polygon deep links (`buildAsfVertexDeepLink`) and `asf_search` Python download snippets (`buildAsfDownloadSnippet`) in `AnalystWorkbench.tsx`. |
| 3 | **NISAR Data User Guide & JPL D-102255 Spec** | NASA / JPL | `https://nisar.jpl.nasa.gov/data/products/` | 18-token single (`GCOV`, `GSLC`, `SME2`) and 20-token paired (`GUNW`, `GOFF`) granule parser in `src/services/nisarCatalogService.ts`; HDF5 `/science/LSAR/...` reader in `pipeline/ingest_nisar.py`; CRID/maturity disclaimers in `src/data/nisarProducts.ts` and `src/components/DataPassport.tsx`. |
| 4 | **What is Remote Sensing?** | NASA Earthdata | `https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/earth-observation-data-basics/remote-sensing` | Active vs. passive microwave physics foundation in Beat 1 of `src/components/StoryScrollyModal.tsx` and Tab 1 (`SAR 101`) of `src/components/SciencePrimerModal.tsx`. |
| 5 | **What is Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)?** | NASA Earthdata | `https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/earth-observation-data-basics/sar` | Powers the 5 interactive physics mini-demos in `src/components/SciencePrimerModal.tsx` (coherent speckle ENL, side-looking layover/foreshortening, dielectric permittivity vs moisture/freeze–thaw, surface roughness vs $\lambda$, and InSAR phase wrapping). |
| 6 | **NASA ARSET SAR Training (English & Spanish)** | NASA ARSET | `https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/what-we-do/capacity-building/arset` | Methodological basis for log-ratio dB differencing ($10\log_{10}(I_2/I_1)$), Stable-ROI noise calibration, and bilingual Spanish (`es`) UI support in `src/i18n/translations.ts`. |
| 7 | **NISAR Science StoryMaps (5 Themes)** | NASA / JPL | `https://nisar.jpl.nasa.gov/science/overview/` | Mapped 1-to-1 across our 5 regional biomes (Rasuwa Solid Earth/Hydrology, Pantanal Wetlands/Ecosystems, San Joaquin Agriculture/Subsidence, Hatay Solid Earth/Earthquakes, Alaska–Yukon Cryosphere) and tagged via `nisarStoryMapTheme` in `src/science/hypotheses.ts`. |
| 8 | **NISAR Applications White Papers** | NASA / JPL | `https://nisar.jpl.nasa.gov/science/applications/` | Synthesized in Tab 5 (`Future of NISAR & White Papers`) of `src/components/SciencePrimerModal.tsx` and `docs/submission/FUTURE_NISAR_APPLICATIONS.md`, detailing synergy with OPERA, GEDI, SMAP, GRACE-FO, SWOT, and ICESat-2. |
| 9 | **NASA JPL UAVSAR & Polarimetric Postcards** | NASA / JPL | `https://uavsar.jpl.nasa.gov/` | Integrated in Tab 2 (`Polarization Explorer` — Quad-Pol Pauli decomposition) and Tab 3 (`Sensor Rosetta` — `NASA_UAVSAR_L` profile) of `src/components/SciencePrimerModal.tsx` and `src/science/sensorRosetta.ts`. |
| 10 | **CSA RADARSAT Constellation Mission (RCM)** | CSA (Canada) | `https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/satellites/radarsat/` | Modeled in `src/science/sensorRosetta.ts` (`CSA_RCM_C`: C-band 5.6 cm, 4-day constellation repeat, compact polarization) and recommended by `recommendSensorsForTask()` whenever sub-weekly revisit is needed. |
| 11 | **CSA SAR Data Processing Python Tutorial** | CSA (Canada) | `https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/satellites/radarsat/technical-features/sar-tutorial.asp` | Integrated into `pipeline/notebooks/sar_change_tutorial.ipynb` and `pipeline/ingest_nisar.py` for radiometric dB scaling, speckle filtering, and log-ratio change detection. |
| 12 | **CSA RADARSAT-1 Open Historical Archive** | CSA (Canada) | `https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/satellites/radarsat1/` | Modeled in `src/science/sensorRosetta.ts` (`CSA_RADARSAT1_C`: 1995–2013 open HH archive) and recommended by `recommendSensorsForTask()` when 10–30 year historical baselines are required. |
| 13 | **CONAE SAOCOM 1A/1B L-Band Catalog** | CONAE (Argentina) | `https://catalogos.conae.gov.ar/catalogo/catalogoSat.html` | Modeled in `src/science/sensorRosetta.ts` (`CONAE_SAOCOM_L`: 23.5 cm L-band quad-pol, 8-day constellation repeat) and documented with a complete access workflow in `docs/SAOCOM_REQUEST_GUIDE.md` and `src/components/ResourcesDrawer.tsx`. |
