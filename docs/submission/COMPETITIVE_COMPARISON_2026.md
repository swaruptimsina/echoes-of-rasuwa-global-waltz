## 3. Where Competitors Excel & What We Learned (Respectful Benchmarking)

An honest competitive review requires crediting where rival 2026 teams solved real technical problems better or differently than we did — and being transparent about our own architectural boundaries:

1. **TERRAPULSE's Live HTTP HDF5 Byte-Range Chunk Streaming & STAC 1.1.0 Catalog:**
   - *Why it shines:* Rather than downloading multi-gigabyte `GCOV` granules in full, *TerraPulse* parses the HDF5 chunk index and issues parallel HTTP range requests (`fsspec` / `aiohttp`) to fetch only AOI-intersecting compressed chunks (checked against `h5py`), reducing 6–9 GB files to `0.7–2 MB` browser rasters and exporting a `pystac`-validated **STAC 1.1.0** catalog. Their `57`-test CI suite with mutation testing (`Counterproof`) and automatic lake/wind/rain QC flagging sets a gold standard for hydrological time-series engineering.
   - *Where Echoes of Rasuwa differs cleanly:* Our browser app does **not** perform raw remote HTTP HDF5 byte-range chunking inside browser RAM (which would hit NASA Earthdata Login credential/CORS constraints for zero-login judges). Instead, we pair: (a) **live unauthenticated NASA CMR metadata & public ASF `_LATLON.png` quicklooks**, (b) **offline range-read HDF5 subsetting** (`public/rasuwa-datum/`, `pipeline/ingest_nisar.py`) producing real bundled multi-band GeoTIFFs (`nisar_event_source_gcov_change.tif`, `nisar_quiet_source_gcov_change.tif`, `nisar_event_source_gunw.tif`, `opera_t085_event_vv_change_db.tif`, `copernicus_glo30.tif`) that **decode natively in the browser via `geotiff`** (`RasuwaDatumExplorer.tsx` & `AnalystWorkbench.tsx`), and (c) local user `.tif` / COG URL inspection (`inspectGeoTiffFromFile`, `inspectCogFromUrl`). Adding direct Earthdata-proxied Zarr/`VirtualiZarr` chunk streaming and STAC 1.1.0 output to `pipeline/ingest_nisar.py` is a concrete Phase-2 enhancement noted in [`LIMITATIONS_AND_NEXT_STEPS.md`](./LIMITATIONS_AND_NEXT_STEPS.md).

2. **Stillwater's 11-Pass Persistence Screening & Optical Cloud Audit:**
   - *Why it shines:* *Stillwater* directly answers a concrete local planner need (WWF / agricultural extension rice–fish surveys) using `11` consecutive `Track 47` NISAR passes, requiring ≥3 consecutive passes (`≥24 days`) to filter transient tilled-field false positives, correcting an empirical `+0.5 dB` inter-CRID calibration jump (`P05036` vs `P05023`), and quantifying optical blindness (`2/51` and `0/54` usable HLS scenes) on the exact study frames.
   - *Where Echoes of Rasuwa differs:* We investigate a **rapid, steep-relief Himalayan cascading hazard** (`2026-08-26T02:52:10Z` Rasuwa–Bhotekoshi event) where L-band phase collapsed (`9/528` pixels) rather than a flat-delta multi-month inundation pulse — demonstrating both *when* NISAR L-band phase fails and *why* C-band Sentinel-1D coherence (`0.5129` → `0.3950`) and 3-view OPERA RTC-S1 (`1.1871 km²`) must complement L-band in steep gorges.

3. **SPECTRA-SAR's 5-Stage Mission Review Studio:**
   - *Why it shines:* *0xNEBULA*'s **Observe → Validate → Detect → Prioritize → Prove** workflow cleanly mirrors operational analyst triage and candidly avoids conflating Alaska NISAR test data with Spain Sen1Floods11 ML benchmarks.
   - *Where Echoes of Rasuwa differs:* We embed that same provenance discipline directly into UI architecture via **`<DataPassport />`** (with compile/lint-enforced separation between `REAL_NISAR_PRODUCT`, `NASA_PUBLISHED_BROWSE`, `CATALOG_METADATA_LIVE`, `CATALOG_SNAPSHOT`, `MODEL_OUTPUT`, `REPORTED_ESTIMATE`, `ILLUSTRATION`, `SIMULATION`, and `TEST_FIXTURE`) and extend it to non-specialists through Lali's narrative and playable missions.

4. **CHRONO-SAR's 3D Interferometric Terrain Drape & In-Browser Unwrapping Simulator:**
   - *Why it shines:* *CyberPulse* makes abstract InSAR phase fringes intuitive by draping `Δd = λ/4π · Δφ` directly onto 3D elevation models across 6 global hazards and benchmarking an in-browser phase-unwrapping algorithm (`4.3 mm` error).
   - *Where Echoes of Rasuwa differs:* While our `TerrainMap3D` Three.js Valley Lab renders 3D terrain across 5 global biomes with interactive pass sliders and explicit disclosure of its artistic LOS height warp, we emphasize **when interferometric unwrapping fails in real mountain data** (`9/528 = 1.70%` connected pixels in Rasuwa NISAR GUNW vs `420/420` control) and cross-validate C-band coherence loss against an independent preliminary UNOSAT detachment crown.

5. **4/K's Pre-Comparison Compatibility Gate & Lắng's Folk-Instrument Voice Accessibility:**
   - *Why they shine:* *NISAR ChangeLens* prevents invalid track/geometry/polarization subtractions before rendering a Δγ⁰ map (`99.9065%` valid-pixel overlap check), just as our `rankPrePostEventPairs()` in `nisarCatalogService.ts` pairs matching track/frame/look-direction passes and our `RasuwaDatumExplorer` renders common-mask GCOV/OPERA subsets. Meanwhile, *Lắng* sets the accessibility ceiling with Vietnamese voice commands and folk-instrument sonification; our **3/4 Waltz Web Audio sonification** + **neural female narration** (`Edge AnaNeural` / `Gemini Zephyr`) brings multimodal audio-first literacy directly into the SAR domain.

## 4. Why *"Echoes of Rasuwa: Global Waltz"* Stands Out on the 5 NASA Judging Criteria

Per the official [2026 Judging & Awards Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/), projects are scored 1–5 across **Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, and Presentation**. Most top submissions excel at *one* pole — either technical/hydrological engineering (*TerraPulse*, *Stillwater*, *SPECTRA-SAR*, *CHRONO-SAR*) **or** accessible storytelling/education (*Zumorroda-X* in 2025, *Lắng* in 2026). **Echoes of Rasuwa: Global Waltz** is uniquely positioned because it delivers **both** without compromising either:

### 1. Impact — From Mountain Village Drills to Global Climate Literacy
- Connects orbital SAR physics to real community infrastructure decisions across **five Himalayan field stops** (`1,460 m` Syabrubesi Flag House, `1,640 m` Chilime 111 MW Hydro Intake, `1,760 m` Timure Dry Port, `1,814 m` Rasuwagadhi & Miteri Bridge, plus the DHM 3D Science Lab) and **four global biome missions** (San Joaquin groundwater subsidence, Pantanal wetland canopy double-bounce, Hatay fault/dam triage, and Arctic oil/Hubbard Glacier calving).
- Bridges K-12/university education and operational awareness via a ready-to-teach [45-Minute NGSS Lesson Plan](../educator/LESSON_PLAN_45MIN.md), multilingual UI (`EN`, `NE Draft`, `ES Draft`), and explicit ethical guidance warning against treating SAR screening as an automated evacuation siren (`Zenodo 22730419` false-positive triage dilemma).

### 2. Creativity — Zumorroda-X-Caliber Emotional Adventure Meets Multimodal Sonification
- **Lali's 9-Chapter Guided Expedition** (`RasuwaStoryAdventure.tsx`) pairs handcrafted pixel-art scenes, before/after photo sliders, 8 interactive hazard mini-games (`RasuwaPlayableMissions.tsx`), and 14 inspectable Proof Stories with **neural voice narration** (`msedge-tts` `en-US-AnaNeural` + optional `Gemini` TTS in `server.ts`, with offline fallback) — translating the emotional, character-led magic that won *Zumorroda-X* the 2025 *Art & Technology* Global Award into NISAR radar science.
- **3/4 Orbital Waltz Sonification** (`StoryScrollyModal.tsx`) turns 6 sequential 12-day SAR passes into an audible 3/4 waltz measure (HH backscatter pitch + HV canopy chord + pure fifth when coherent `γ ≥ 0.50` vs dissonant tritone `1.414×` when decorrelated), accompanied by full acoustic text transcripts.

### 3. Validity — Radical Scientific Honesty & Negative-Result Accounting
No inspected 2026 competitor matches our breadth of **quantified negative-result reporting and empirical error accounting** over a real steep-terrain disaster (`2026-08-26T02:52:10Z` Rasuwa–Bhotekoshi):
- **Diagnosed NISAR L-Band GUNW & GOFF Failures (`l_vs_c_coherence.json`, `subtle_change_results.json`):** Explicitly proves that NISAR L-band median coherence at the upper source collapsed from `0.2188` to `0.1021` with connected unwrapped phase surviving in only **`9 / 528` pixels (`1.70%`)** (vs **`420 / 420`** in the spatial control and `0` source pixels on Track 48), and raw `GOFF` offsets were outlier-dominated (`0.2206` correlation) — therefore **refusing** to emit a fabricated NISAR displacement map.
- **Cross-Sensor C-Band & False-Alarm Calibration (`coherence_loss_screening_mask.json`):** Documents Sentinel-1D HyP3 coherence loss inside the `2.53 km²` preliminary UNOSAT detachment (`0.5129` → `0.3950`, target Δ `-0.1313` vs control ring `+0.1841`), flagging **`158 / 307` (`51.5%`)** target pixels alongside **`1,225 / 30,161` (`4.06%`)** control false alarms, and **OPERA L2 RTC-S1** flagging **`1.1871 km²`** across only **`48.15%`** 2-view observable steep terrain (`>70°` masked).
- **Trigger Falsification & Archive Latency Audit (`rainfall_timeseries.json`, `latency_table.json`):** Shows native half-hour GPM IMERG Early & Late runs recorded **`0.0 ± 0.24 mm/hr`** at the `02:30–02:59 UTC` collapse window (falsifying an immediate local cloudburst trigger) and publishes empirical CMR archive insertion delays (**`+3.25d`** Sentinel-1D SLC/burst, **`+4.03d`–`+4.14d`** NISAR provisional L2, **`+46.38d`** OPERA RTC-S1, **`+47.23d`** OPERA DSWx-S1).
- **Cryptographic & Automated Guardrails:** `DataPassport` gating + `59` Node tests (`npm test`) + `2` Python pipeline tests (`pipeline/tests/test_pipeline.py`) + automated claim-integrity linter (`scripts/check-claims.mjs`, `0` violations).

### 4. Relevance — Full NASA + Space Agency Partner Fleet Integration
- Directly integrates **NASA–ISRO NISAR** (`GCOV`, `GUNW`, `GOFF`, `GSLC`, `SME2`, `UR_L2`; `6,803` exact-AOI CMR granules in the Rasuwa system datum + `2,667` across 6 global AOIs in `cmr-snapshot-2026-11-13.json`), the official NASA Earth Observatory **Hubbard Glacier Mw 7.0** reference pair (`2025-11-26` / `2025-12-08`), **OPERA RTC-S1**, **GPM IMERG**, **NASA POWER**, **EONET v3**, **Copernicus Sentinel-1D & GLO-30 DEM**, **CSA RCM & RADARSAT-1**, and **CONAE SAOCOM 1A/1B** (`sensorRosetta.ts`, `pipeline/ingest_nisar.py`).

### 5. Presentation — 3-Door UX, Zero-Friction Judge Paths & Reproducible Engineering
