# Tough Judge Q&A Preparation (`JUDGES_QA.md`)

### Q1 (SAR Physicist Judge): *"Are the rasters in your browser workbench real calibrated NISAR Level-2 GCOV/GUNW float32 arrays or synthetic fixtures?"*
- **Direct Answer:** Both are supported and strictly separated by our **`DataPassport`** gate (`src/data/nisarProducts.ts`):
  1. **In the zero-credential browser deployment:** We query **real NISAR Level-2/3 granule metadata and public ASF `_LATLON.png` browse quicklooks (`PUBLIC_CATALOG_METADATA`)** from the NASA CMR API (316 hits over Rasuwa, 624 over Hubbard Glacier) and display **real NASA Earth Observatory published NISAR imagery (`NASA_PUBLISHED_REFERENCE`)** for the Nov 26 / Dec 8, 2025 Hubbard Glacier earthquake pair. Because raw NISAR HDF5 granules are 400 MB to >4 GB and require Earthdata `.netrc` authentication, the interactive pixel-level dB & LOS calibration grid in the browser defaults to a deterministic, physically grounded **`TEST_FIXTURE`** (`generateDeterministicAoiRasters`) explicitly badged as `[⚙ SYNTHETIC WORKBENCH TEST FIXTURE]`.
  2. **In local HDF5 mode:** Running `python3 pipeline/ingest_nisar.py --hdf5 <granule.h5>` reads `/science/LSAR/GCOV/grids/frequencyA/HHHH` or `GUNW` unwrapped phase, converts linear power to calibrated dB ($10\log_{10}(\gamma^0)$), and writes a schema-validated `manifest.json` that unlocks the `[★ REAL NISAR LEVEL-2/3 PRODUCT]` badge.

### Q2 (InSAR Specialist Judge): *"How do you avoid claiming absolute vertical uplift or subsidence from a single Line-of-Sight interferogram?"*
- **Direct Answer:** In `sampleLosTransect()` (`src/utils/changeDetection.ts`) and the Analyst Workbench Transect chart, every displacement value is computed **relative to a user-selected Reference Pixel $R$** ($\Delta d_{\text{LOS,rel}} = d_{\text{LOS}}(x,y) - d_{\text{LOS}}(R)$) and explicitly labeled as **1D Radar Line-of-Sight (LOS)** motion. Users can click **Shift Reference Pixel (R)** to see the entire profile shift vertically, demonstrating why InSAR is a relative LOS measurement that requires multi-geometry (ascending + descending) inversion or GNSS tying to resolve vertical vs. horizontal components.

### Q3 (Remote Sensing Methodologist Judge): *"Why did you implement log-ratio dB differencing and Stable-ROI calibration instead of simple image subtraction?"*
- **Direct Answer:** Coherent SAR speckle is multiplicative in linear power ($I = \sigma^0 \cdot n$), meaning linear subtraction ($I_2 - I_1$) produces high false-alarm rates in bright foreshortened terrain. Taking the log-ratio in decibels ($\Delta\sigma^0_{\text{dB}} = 10\log_{10}(I_2/I_1)$) transforms multiplicative speckle into additive noise. We then compute the empirical mean and standard deviation ($\mu_{\text{ROI}}, \sigma_{\text{ROI}}$) inside a **Stable Reference ROI** (`computeRoiStats`) to set $T_{\text{dB}} = |\mu_{\text{ROI}}| + k\sigma_{\text{ROI}}$ and apply a 4-neighbor connected-components **Minimum Mapping Unit (MMU)** filter before computing changed area $\pm$ uncertainty.

### Q4 (Multi-Agency Space Apps Judge): *"How did you integrate Canadian Space Agency (CSA) and CONAE (Argentina) SAR resources alongside NASA NISAR?"*
- **Direct Answer:** In `src/science/sensorRosetta.ts`, `src/components/SciencePrimerModal.tsx`, `src/components/ResourcesDrawer.tsx`, and `docs/SAOCOM_REQUEST_GUIDE.md`, we model and compare all 8 sensors (**NISAR L-band**, **NISAR S-band**, **Sentinel-1 C-band**, **CSA RCM C-band**, **CSA RADARSAT-1 archive**, **CONAE SAOCOM 1A/1B L-band**, **JAXA ALOS-2 L-band**, and **NASA UAVSAR**). Our rule-based recommender (`recommendSensorsForTask`) directs users to **CSA RCM** when 4-day rapid revisit or compact polarization is needed, **CSA RADARSAT-1** when 30-year (1995–2026) historical baselines are needed, and **CONAE SAOCOM** when 8-day South American L-band quad-pol synergy (e.g., over the Pantanal) is needed.

### Q5 (Disaster Preparedness / Ethics Judge): *"Does your app claim to predict earthquakes or issue operational flood evacuation warnings?"*
- **Direct Answer:** Never. We enforce an automated **Scientific Claim-Integrity Linter** (`scripts/check-claims.mjs`, run on every `npm run verify`) that blocks any claim of operational early warning, disaster timing prediction, or unverified casualty/financial statistics. Every hypothesis card lists mandatory physical confounders and falsification criteria.
